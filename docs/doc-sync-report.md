### `docs/SECURITY-OPS.md` §C — shipped pinning mechanism diverges from the documented design
The runbook's steps 4–5 (pre-existing, unedited) call for gating pinning behind an **OTA/remote-config
kill-switch** and rolling out to a **canary cohort first**. What actually shipped
(`with-certificate-pinning.js` + `MOBILE-CERT-PINNING.md`) uses a **build-time env var**
(`LYNIA_TLS_PINS`) instead — by the plugin's own documentation, "not OTA-able" and with no canary
mechanism; rollback requires a full native rebuild. This may be a deliberate, documented simplification
(the Android pin-expiration safety valve substitutes for a kill-switch), but it contradicts what the
design doc prescribes. Left both docs as-is rather than silently rewrite the design intent — a human
should decide whether to (a) update `SECURITY-OPS.md` §C steps 4–5 to match the simpler shipped design,
or (b) treat the missing kill-switch/canary as a gap to build before arming in production.
