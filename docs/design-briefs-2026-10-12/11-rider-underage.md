# 11 · Under 18: a final "not yet" screen

**App:** Rider. **Extends:** first-run-v2 **F5** (locked) and the F4 decline family.

## The problem

The ID check declines anyone under 18 (all countries). The app now records this as its own reason, `underage`, and treats it as **final**: trying again can't change a birth date. Today the rider lands on F5 "Let's finish this together · Both tries are used. Our team will check your ID with you on WhatsApp". That screen is wrong for them. It suggests a person can fix it, and it says they used two tries when they used one.

## Must

- Say plainly that riders must be **18 or older**. No blame, no mention of a birth date or any document detail.
- **No Try again.** Nothing on the screen should restart the check.
- Leave the rest of the app open: they can still order food and send parcels (`KY.secondary`).
- The ✕ goes to customer Home, like every F state (D-82).
- Calm, not alarming: use the **neutral** or **mint** tone, not danger. They did nothing wrong.

## Your call

- Whether to say when they can come back ("You can apply once you turn 18"). We know their birth date only inside the ID check; we don't store it, so we can't show a date.
- Whether there is a help link at all. Our suggestion: no WhatsApp CTA, since support can't change the outcome.
