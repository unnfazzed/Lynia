# 12 · "This ID is on another account"

**App:** Rider. **Extends:** first-run-v2 **F5dup** (sanctioned in D-82 §4, body not drawn).

## The problem

When the ID on the document is already on another LyniaGo account, the check is declined `duplicate` automatically. It's the one-ID-one-account rule, and it stops banned riders coming back on a new SIM.

But most people who hit it are honest: they **changed their phone number** and signed up again. Today they get a decline and two tries, and both tries will fail the same way. That is a dead end.

## Must

- Say the ID is **already on another LyniaGo account**. Never show which account, its number, or any part of it: that would leak someone's phone number to whoever holds a copy of their ID.
- Give the two ways forward:
  1. **Sign in with your old number** (goes to sign-in; the old account keeps its history).
  2. **Message us** (`KY.msg`, WhatsApp) for a lost number. Support moves the account.
- **No Try again.** Retrying with the same ID can't pass.
- ✕ to customer Home (D-82).

## Your call

- Which of the two is primary. We'd guess "Sign in with your old number".
- Tone. Danger is wrong for the honest majority; neutral or mint is probably right.

## Copy to start from

- Reason label: "Already registered on another account".
- `KY.msg` "Message us on WhatsApp" · `KY.help` "Help on WhatsApp".

Suggested: "This ID is on" / "another account" · body "Sign in with the number you used before, or message us and we'll move it."
