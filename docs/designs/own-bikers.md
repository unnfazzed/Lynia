# Design: Own bikers (merchant-owned fleets)

Generated by /office-hours on 2026-10-19
Branch: claude/tender-euler-mhwajf
Repo: unnfazzed/lynia
Status: APPROVED
Mode: Startup (intrapreneurship)

## Problem Statement

Some merchants (restaurants, shops, pharmacies) employ their own bikers. Today Lynia only knows them as
"Preferred riders": a phone-number list (`MerchantPreferredRider`, migration 0055) that reorders
dispatch (`PREFERRED_DISPATCH_SLACK_M`, 2 km) and adds a ranking bonus in Book a biker
(`PREFERRED_OFFER_BONUS = 0.5`). Preferred bikers remain public bikers who take anyone's work and pay
the standard biker commission.

The owner wants to replace "Preferred" with **Own bikers**: a merchant's own fleet that takes **only
that merchant's orders** (never public parcels, never other merchants), with Lynia charging a
**customised fee per merchant** on those rides.

## Demand Evidence

- Thin, and named as such. Fleet merchants exist and dispatch their own bikers **offline**: orders arrive
  by phone or WhatsApp, and a manager phones a biker.
- The pain chosen was **dispatch chaos** (jobs missed, doubled or late while one person coordinates by
  phone). It was picked from options. Nobody has observed it or quoted it.
- **No merchant has been named yet. Nobody has watched one dispatch.** See The Assignment.

## Status Quo

The fleet merchant's phone/WhatsApp setup costs them **$0 in fees**. That is the competitor. On these
orders Lynia supplies no customer demand, so Own bikers is **dispatch software for orders Lynia didn't
originate**, plus fleet-first routing for the orders it did.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

- **Target user:** the person running deliveries at a fleet merchant. That person is not named yet.
- **Wedge:** the merchant enters a phone order in the existing Book a biker flow, and it rings **only
  their Own bikers**, with the existing 6-digit codes and cash proof.
- The approach chosen (below) builds this wedge and the rest of the product together.

## Constraints

- **Cash only.** No payment rail. Wallet top-ups are credited by admin by hand.
- **Pixel parity** (CLAUDE.md). Every new or changed screen needs a design handoff. The current
  merchant-v2 Account draws a "Preferred riders" row, so renaming it without a new export is a
  deviation.
- **The board is one nationwide WebSocket room** (`BOARD_ROOM`, D-99). It can't be filtered per biker.
- **Commission is one global env rate** (`COMMISSION_RATE_PCT`), taken from the biker's prepaid wallet in
  `WalletService.chargeCommission`. There is no per-merchant or per-rider override and no merchant
  wallet.
- **Copy:** user-visible text says "biker" (D-101).

## Premises (agreed; #5 revised after the second opinion)

1. **Own biker is a hard link with consent.** A biker belongs to at most one merchant, via invite plus
   the biker accepting. The phone-number preferred list is retired. Existing preferred entries are
   **not** auto-converted, because turning a biker exclusive cuts off their public income.
2. **Exclusivity runs both ways, enforced on the server.** An Own biker gets no public board, no public
   push and no other merchant's dispatch, and every accept/bid/pick path refuses cross-traffic. Hiding it
   in the UI is not enough.
3. **A fleet booking is a direct offer, not an auction.** It rings Own bikers only. There is no fare and
   no bidding. Codes and cash proof are kept. If no Own biker takes it, the merchant can explicitly fall
   back to public (today's booking).
4. **Lynia marketplace orders at a fleet merchant** go to the Own fleet first, then to public bikers.
5. **(Revised)** A **flat fee per ride, set per merchant by admin**, recorded on a **merchant fleet
   ledger** and **billed weekly in arrears**.
   - There is no hard block. Admin can pause fleet mode for a merchant who doesn't pay.
   - Own bikers pay nothing on their merchant's jobs. The 10 free jobs don't apply.
   - A prepaid merchant wallet is deferred until a merchant depends on the tool.
6. **Own bikers still pass Lynia KYC and standing rules**, because they carry Lynia codes to Lynia
   customers.
7. **The UI needs a design handoff before build.**

## Landscape

- Marketplaces roughly halve their cut for self-delivery: DoorDash takes 30% on marketplace orders and
  15% on self-delivery, because the platform still supplies the customer.
- Pure dispatch SaaS (Shipday, DataDreamers, DeliverLogic) charges about $0.10–0.25 per order or
  $39–$99 a month.
- **EUREKA:** with no fare there is nothing to take a percentage of, and Lynia brings no customer to a
  phone order. So the "custom commission" is a **flat fee per ride**.

## Recommended Approach — B: Own bikers, complete (owner's choice)

### Data model (Prisma)

- **`Merchant.fleetEnabled`** (bool, admin), **`Merchant.fleetRideFee`** (Decimal, admin) and
  **`Merchant.fleetPausedAt`** (admin pause for non-payment).
- **`Rider.fleetMerchantId`** (nullable FK, unique per rider by construction) and **`Rider.fleetJoinedAt`**.
  - This is the single source of truth for exclusivity.
  - It is a real FK, not phone-matched, because exclusivity is enforcement, not ordering.
- **Fleet invite:**
  - Reuse `MerchantInvite` with a `kind` (`staff` | `biker`), or add a sibling `MerchantFleetInvite`
    (decide in eng review).
  - Pending until the biker taps Join. First Join wins.
  - A biker already in another fleet cannot join a second one.
- **`MerchantFleetLedger`**: merchantId, orderId (unique), riderId, fee, kind (`booking` |
  `marketplace`), createdAt, statementId.
- **`MerchantFleetStatement`**: merchantId, periodStart/End, total, status (`open` | `sent` | `paid`),
  paidAt, recordedByProfileId. One per week, closed by admin.
- **`Order.fleetMerchantId`** (nullable): set on a fleet booking at creation, and on a marketplace order
  when an Own biker is assigned. **`Order.fleetFee`** (Decimal, nullable): the merchant's `fleetRideFee`
  **snapshotted** at that same moment, so a mid-week fee change never re-prices rides already made.
- **`MerchantPreferredRider`**: **Preferred is removed for every merchant** (owner, D6, 2026-10-19).
  - The table stays read-only for history.
  - The "Preferred riders" Account row, the `PREFERRED_OFFER_BONUS` / "Your rider" tag, and the
    preferred-first dispatch block are deleted for all merchants, fleet or not.
  - Non-fleet merchants see no rider row at all. Their orders go to the nearest eligible bikers.
  - When a merchant is fleet-enabled, its old preferred phones are shown once as "Invite as Own biker?"
    suggestions.
  - This removes a drawn merchant-v2 row, so it needs the D-ledger entry and the Account redraw in the
    handoff.

### Exclusivity: every door, server-side

One helper, `common/fleet-scope.ts`, beside `commission-cover.ts`. It answers two questions:
- "May rider R serve order O?"
- "Which riders are fleet-locked?"

Call sites from the code map:

**Public parcel board and push**
- Exclude fleet riders from `listOpen` / `listOpenNearby` (`orders.service.ts:716,771`).
- Exclude them from FCM recipients in `broadcastToNearbyRiders` (`orders.service.ts:458-485`) and
  `expandBroadcast` (`matching.service.ts:~385`).
- Refuse them in `boardSubscribe` (`tracking.gateway.ts:348`).
  - Fleet riders never join `BOARD_ROOM`. They get their own `fleet:<merchantId>` room instead.
  - This sidesteps filtering the shared room.

**Bid, list and pick**
- `makeOffer` (`offers.service.ts:~120-155`) refuses a fleet rider on any non-fleet order.
- `listForOrder` (`offers.service.ts:370`) drops them.
- `selectOffer` (`matching.service.ts:111-126`) re-checks.

**Food dispatch**
- `pickCandidates` takes a fleet scope:
  - Other merchants' fleet riders are always excluded.
  - For this merchant: Own fleet first, then public.
- This replaces the preferred-first block (`dispatch-strategy.ts:111-135`).
- `acceptDispatch` (`food-dispatch.service.ts:665-689`) re-checks the scope.
  - While there, close the gap the code map found: `acceptDispatch` does not re-check
    `hasOpenMerchantObligation`.

**More doors**
- Admin manual assign/reassign (`admin/admin-orders.service.ts`) goes through the same check.
- `cloneForRebroadcast` carries `fleetMerchantId`, so a clone of a fleet order is still fleet-scoped.
- `GET /orders/demand` (heat) returns nothing to a fleet rider.
- Rider order detail/tracking reads only return orders the rider is assigned to or offered.

**One choke point under all of it (the guarantee, not the door list)**
- A Postgres trigger on `orders` refuses any write that sets `rider_id`, or sets `fleet_merchant_id`,
  unless **both** hold:
  1. The rider's `fleet_merchant_id` equals the order's `fleet_merchant_id`, comparing NULL to NULL as
     equal.
  2. A non-null order `fleet_merchant_id` equals the merchant the order **belongs to**:
     - `orders.merchant_id` for a marketplace order
     - `merchant_bookings.merchant_id` for a booking
- So a write that copies the rider's link onto some other merchant's order is still refused.
- **Condition 1 applies only when `rider_id` is non-null after the write.** With no rider (fleet booking
  creation, conversion, unassign) only condition 2 is checked.
- It is a **`DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED` constraint trigger**, checked at commit. That way a booking's
  `orders` row and its `merchant_bookings` row, written in one transaction, are both visible when it
  runs.
- That covers both directions:
  - a fleet rider on a public or other-merchant order
  - a public rider on a fleet order
- A future code path that forgets `fleet-scope` fails at the database instead of leaking work.
- The trigger lives in the migration with a spec that tries each forbidden write.
- The app-level doors stay, so users get a clean error instead of a DB exception.

**Which gates apply to fleet jobs**
- A job is a fleet job for rider R when `R.fleetMerchantId` is non-null **and** one of:
  - `order.fleetMerchantId === R.fleetMerchantId` (a fleet booking, or an assigned marketplace order)
  - `order.orderType === "merchant" && order.merchantId === R.fleetMerchantId` (the merchant's
    marketplace order **before** assignment: the fleet-first round and `acceptDispatch`)
- **Still apply:** KYC/standing (`onlineRefusalReason`), one active ride, blocks, and
  `hasOpenMerchantObligation`.
- **Skipped, for fleet jobs only:** commission cover (`assertRiderCoversCommission`,
  `ridersWhoCoverCommission`). The biker owes Lynia nothing on them, so a zero-balance Own biker can still
  take their merchant's jobs.
- The bypass is a pure function of the order/rider pair, with a spec proving it never applies to a
  non-fleet order.

**Joining and leaving with work in flight**
- **Join is refused** while the biker:
  - has an active ride
  - has an open merchant obligation or cash debt to another merchant ("Finish your job / settle cash
    first")
- **On Join:**
  - The biker's open parcel offers are withdrawn.
  - Every socket of theirs is removed from `BOARD_ROOM` (`socketsLeave`) and joined to
    `fleet:<merchantId>`.
  - The prepaid commission balance is untouched. Fleet jobs don't use it, and it's there if they leave.
- **Leave and Remove are refused** while the biker holds an active fleet job ("after this delivery").
- **On Leave/Remove:**
  - The link clears and the sockets move back to normal board eligibility.
  - The biker gets a push: "You're a public biker again".
- **Scope is checked at offer and assignment** (the trigger). **Billing follows the order's snapshot**
  (`fleetMerchantId`, `fleetFee`), never the biker's current link.

**Tests**
- A spec per door asserting a fleet rider can't see, bid, be picked, be admin-assigned or accept a public
  or other-merchant job.
- A spec that a public rider can't accept a fleet booking.
- Trigger specs.
- Join/Leave transition specs: active ride, debt, open offers, socket rooms.

### Fleet booking (phone orders)

**Modelling (decided)**
- A fleet booking is today's booking order: `orderType: "parcel"`, a `MerchantBooking` row, pickup at
  the merchant's pin.
- It adds `fleetMerchantId` and `fleetFee`, and carries **no fare**: `proposedFare`/`agreedFare` 0.

**Choosing the audience**
- From Book a biker, a fleet-enabled merchant chooses **"My bikers"** (default) or **"Any biker"**
  (today's public auction, unchanged).

**How a "My bikers" booking is offered**
- Until an Own biker accepts, it holds a **new `OrderStatus` value, `fleet_searching`**.
  - This is not `requested`, which means the merchant kitchen phase everywhere in the code.
  - Adding the enum value makes every exhaustive status switch fail to compile until it handles the
    value. The status-keyed query audit (A-11) gets a row for it.
  - The board and `listOpen` only read `open_for_offers`, so it is never on the board.
- It is offered by the `FoodDispatchAttempt` round machinery to the merchant's online, eligible Own
  bikers only. The first to accept wins. The CAS requires `status = 'fleet_searching' AND rider_id IS NULL
  AND fleet_merchant_id = <merchant>`.
- **An Own biker drops an accepted booking before pickup** (or a timeout or admin reassign does it):
  - Status goes back to `fleet_searching` with `rider_id` NULL.
  - `fleet_merchant_id` and `fleet_fee` are **kept**.
  - Rounds restart without that biker, and the merchant can still Send to any biker.

**Paths that must accept a zero fare (each gets a fleet branch + spec)**
- the `proposedFare` minimum validation
- `commissionBasis` / `jobCommissionBasis` (skipped entirely for fleet)
- `quoteFare` prefill (not shown)
- receipts and history ("Paid by <merchant>" instead of a fare)
- biker earnings totals (fleet jobs excluded from fare earnings, counted as "<Merchant> jobs")
- admin fare-adjust (refused on fleet orders)

**Cash flow of a phone order**
- The customer pays the **merchant's** money in cash to the biker: the goods amount plus whatever
  delivery charge the merchant itself sets. This is the existing `collectCash`/COD line, entered by the
  merchant.
- The biker hands all of it back to the merchant, and `closeCash` reconciles it exactly as today.
- Lynia's fleet fee is not in this cash at all. It is billed to the merchant weekly.

**"Send to any biker" (conversion), atomic**
- After N rounds with no taker the merchant sees "None of your bikers took it" with **Send to any biker**.
- Conversion is one transaction on the **same order** (same id, same codes):
  1. A conditional update `SET fleet_merchant_id = NULL, fleet_fee = NULL, proposed_fare = <quoteFare,
     editable>, status = 'open_for_offers' WHERE id = ? AND status = 'fleet_searching' AND rider_id IS NULL
     AND fleet_merchant_id = ?`.
     - A cancelled booking, including one that pause cancelled, no longer matches, so a stale tap can't
       resurrect it.
     - Pause-cancel uses the same `status = 'fleet_searching' AND rider_id IS NULL` guard.
  2. Every pending `FoodDispatchAttempt` for the order is closed.
- An Own biker's accept and the conversion race on the same row condition. Exactly one wins, and the
  loser gets "already taken / already sent to any biker".
- After conversion it is an ordinary public booking: normal biker commission, and no fleet ledger row.
- A spec covers the race.

### Marketplace orders at a fleet merchant

- Dispatch round 1 goes to the merchant's Own bikers within `pickupReachM`, with no 2 km slack. Then
  public riders, as today.
- If an Own biker delivers: write a fleet ledger row at `fleetRideFee`, and **skip** the biker's 10%
  commission debit (`WalletService.chargeCommission`).
- **Unassigning an Own biker before pickup** (they drop it, a dispatch timeout, admin reassign):
  - Clear `fleet_merchant_id` and `fleet_fee` **in the same write** that clears `rider_id`, so dispatch
    can carry on to public riders.
  - The next round re-offers Own bikers first, unless the merchant is paused.
  - A spec covers drop-then-public.
  - After pickup there is no unassign path today, so the snapshot stands.
- If a public biker delivers: normal biker commission, and no merchant fee.

### Commission and billing

- `chargeCommission` skips fleet jobs entirely: no `free_job` row, and no ride commission on the biker.
- Fleet jobs do **not** use up the biker's 10 free jobs, and do **not** count toward `tripsCount` for
  that purpose (decide in eng review whether `tripsCount` still counts them for display).
- Write `MerchantFleetLedger` at the order's **snapshotted** `fleetFee`, idempotent on orderId. When a
  ledger row is written:
  - **Delivered/completed by an Own biker:** yes.
  - **Cancelled after pickup, or undelivered/returned:** yes. The ride happened.
  - **Cancelled before pickup:** no.
  - **Converted to Any biker:** no.
  - **A public biker delivered:** no.
- **Pause** (`fleetPausedAt`, non-payment):
  - "My bikers" is disabled in Book a biker with "Own bikers paused, contact Lynia". "Any biker" still
    works.
  - Marketplace orders skip the fleet-first round.
  - Pending fleet bookings that no one has accepted are cancelled, and the merchant is told.
  - In-flight fleet rides finish and are billed.
  - Own bikers **stay linked and exclusive.** They are the merchant's employees, and pausing doesn't
    release them.
  - A paused merchant's Own bikers are **not candidates in any round** of that merchant's marketplace
    orders, public rounds included. The fleet-job predicate also requires the merchant's `fleetPausedAt`
    to be null. So during a pause no fleet commission is skipped and no ledger row is written.
  - **Unpause** restores everything at once.
- **Disable** (`fleetEnabled` off, the merchant leaves the programme):
  - It does everything pause does.
  - Then every link is dissolved as each biker's in-flight fleet ride ends. Each biker becomes public
    and gets a push.
  - Re-enabling needs fresh invites.
- Weekly statement job: group rows into a `MerchantFleetStatement`.
- Admin money page: set fee, see statement, mark paid, pause/unpause fleet.
- Merchant app: a read-only "This week: N rides · $X" line on the Own bikers screen.

### Biker side

- A fleet biker's Jobs tab shows **"<Merchant> jobs"** instead of the public board.
- There is no public board, no demand heat, and no parcel pushes.
- The Money tab hides top-up prompts for fleet jobs. The biker owes nothing on them.
- Account shows "You ride for <Merchant>" with **Leave**. Leaving needs confirmation and makes them a
  public biker again (the normal wallet rules then apply).
- Merchant side: the owner can **Remove** a biker. That unlinks them, and they become a public biker.

### Merchant side

- For fleet-enabled merchants the Account shows **"Own bikers"**: list, invite by phone, pending invites,
  remove, this week's rides and fee.
- Book a biker gets the My bikers / Any biker choice.
- Only shown when `fleetEnabled` (admin turns it on per merchant).
- Non-fleet merchants: the "Preferred riders" row is gone (D6).

**Invitee journey**
- **Delivery:** the invite goes by SMS to the phone (D-116 SMS channel) with an app link: "<Merchant>
  wants you as their biker on LyniaGo."
- **No Lynia account yet:** they sign up as a biker through first-run-v2 (personal details, bike and
  documents, ID check). The pending invite is matched by phone.
  - The **Join** card appears on their biker Home **only once KYC is verified**.
  - Until then, Join is not offered and their account is not exclusive.
- **Existing biker:** the Join card appears at once. Join runs the transition rules above.
- **Expiry:** an invite expires after 14 days. The merchant can revoke it at any time. First Join wins.
  A biker already in another fleet sees "Leave <Other> first".
- **What the merchant sees per invite:** Invited, Signing up, Waiting for ID check, Joined, Expired.

### R3-1 — feasibility (blocking)

**Problem**

> The trigger now also fires on writes that set fleet_merchant_id, and condition 1 always requires the rider's fleet_merchant_id to equal the order's. The document never says how the trigger treats rider_id IS NULL. That case also covers the conversion write and the new unassign write, which clears rider_id and fleet_merchant_id together. The R2-2 remedy's transition rule is also not stated: fleet_merchant_id may only go NULL→owning merchant (at booking creation or Own-biker assignment) and owning merchant→NULL only with rider_id NULL.

**Remedy**

Add trigger specs for fleet-booking creation (no rider), conversion and the unassign-clears-both write, plus the forbidden cases.

### R3-2 — feasibility (blocking)

**Remedy**

### R3-3 — feasibility (blocking)

**Problem**

In the codebase, requested is the merchant-order kitchen phase (schema comment on MerchantPhase: 'requested (never written by the parcel path…) is the kitchen-side phase'; order-lifecycle.transitions.ts: 'requested … the running code NEVER writes it' for parcels; the status-keyed-query-audit A-11 assumes requested means orderType merchant with a MerchantPhase). **Remedy**
