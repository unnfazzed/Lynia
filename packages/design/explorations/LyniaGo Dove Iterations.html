Paper Dove · iterations

Same bird, five temperaments. Pick by badge (2a–2e).

2a · origami (Japanese)

Origami tsuru

Extra folds — a second wing behind and a head crease. Precise, crafted, paper-first. The most “made by hand”.

2b · minimal

Flat silhouette

One colour, no folds, fewest points. Quietest and most “financial-grade” — ages best, prints anywhere.

2c · rounded

Soft fold

Same geometry, corners rounded — warm and friendly (Chowdeck energy). Best match for the Fredoka wordmark.

2d · the olive leaf ✦

Noah’s delivery (my choice)

The base dove carrying a tiny olive leaf — the first confirmed delivery, completed. The faith layer gets its proof, still whisper-quiet.

2e · the lift

With lift lines

Faint rising trails under the tail — motion made visible. Great for splash/loading; probably too much for the static mark.
