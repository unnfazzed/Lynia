The Paper Dove

At first glance, it’s a send. The folded paper dart every human on a phone already reads as “on its way.” Delivery, understood in a tenth of a second.

Look again — it’s a dove. Raised wing, beak, tail. The messenger bird. Noah’s dove carried the olive leaf back to the ark — the first confirmed delivery in history — and it is the quiet emblem of the Spirit, of peace, of trust. Hidden in plain sight, for those with eyes to see.

And it’s folded paper. The material of the informal economy — banknotes, receipts, notes passed hand to hand. LyniaGo folds that paper into flight: parcels first, then money, credit and dignity, along the same rails.

It climbs to the right. Go. Growth. The last mile, rising.
