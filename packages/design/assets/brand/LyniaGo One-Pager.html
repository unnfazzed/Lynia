LyniaGo
Brand one-pager
v1 · July 2026

LyniaGo is an on-demand motorbike courier for Zimbabwe — you name a price, a nearby rider takes it, and you track the delivery live. The mark is a folded paper dart that is also a dove: the messenger bird carrying the last mile of goods and, in time, of money. LyniaGo is the single consumer brand; Lynia Finance is the background parent company.

The mark — one glyph, four meanings
A send. The paper-dart glyph everyone reads as “on its way.”
A dove. Noah’s dove — the first confirmed delivery; peace, trust.
Folded paper. The material of the informal economy, folded into flight.
The crease is a cross. The fold lines cross at the upper third — hidden in plain sight.

Voice
Second person, sentence case, calm and honest. Every dead-end offers an action. No emoji, no shouting. “No riders took this price yet — nudging it up usually gets a rider fast.”
Do & don’t
Do use the dove on green, white or ink — full lockup at ≥32px.
Don’t add the crease-cross below 32px — use the silhouette.
Do reserve gold for the ‘recommended’ marker only.
Don’t set white text on bright #00B14F — use the CTA green.
Do keep one filled primary button per screen.
Don’t stretch, recolour or rotate the dove.
