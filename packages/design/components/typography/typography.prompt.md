**Typography** — the three text primitives every Lynia screen is built from.

```jsx
<Heading>Send a parcel</Heading>
<Sub>Drop a pin for pickup and drop-off, name your price, and riders will offer.</Sub>
<Label>Your price (USD)</Label>
```

- **Heading** — 24px / 800, one per screen.
- **Sub** — 14px muted lede under the heading.
- **Label** — 12px / 600 muted caption above inputs and small groups.

All Inter. For fares, ETAs and ratings add `className="lynia-tabular"` for lining tabular figures.
