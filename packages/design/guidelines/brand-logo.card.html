Symbolism — one glyph, four layers
A send. The paper-dart glyph the world reads as "on its way" — delivery in a tenth of a second.
A dove. Noah's dove brought the olive leaf home — the first confirmed delivery — and the quiet emblem of the Spirit and of trust.
Folded paper. The material of the informal economy — banknotes, receipts, notes — folded into flight: parcels, then credit.
The crease is a cross. The fold lines cross at the upper third (latin proportions), hidden in plain sight. Files: assets/brand/.
