## Full description (max 4000 chars — this draft ≈1,450)

```
A bike is minutes away.

LyniaGo is an on-demand motorbike courier for Harare. Set your pickup and
drop-off, say what you're sending, and name your price. Nearby riders answer
in seconds — they accept it or counter once — and you choose who carries it.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Set pickup and drop-off on the map
2. Say what you're sending and name your price
3. Riders nearby answer in seconds — accept or counter
4. Compare offers by price, rating and arrival time, then pick your rider
5. Track the delivery live, step by step
6. The recipient gives your delivery code at hand-off — proof it reached
   the right hands

WHY LYNIAGO
• You set the price — no meters, no surprises
• Real riders, real ratings — compare by price, star rating and trips done
• Live tracking from pickup to hand-off
• A delivery code seals every hand-off
• Light on data, made for any Android phone

Documents, pharmacy runs, spare parts, a forgotten charger — whatever needs
to cross town, a rider is already close by.

LyniaGo is launching in Harare, corridor by corridor. If your route isn't
covered yet, it's coming.
```

*(No pricing/promo claims, rankings or award badges — those trigger listing rejections.)*
