# Change: remove the steps card from the splash (2026-10-06)

`Splash.html` is the updated reference. The white steps card at the bottom no longer shows. It used to list "Checking it's you", "Loading your saved places" and "Finding riders near you".

## What changes
- Remove the steps card UI in every phase: boot, loading, offline and done.
- **The boot logic stays the same.** Keep running the three tasks in parallel (session check, saved places, rider/zone availability). `done` still waits for all three plus the 1300ms minimum. With no rows to tick, the per-step 400ms minimum isn't needed any more.
- The slow pill, the offline panel ("Try again" and background retry), the orbit, the sun, the dove, the wordmark and the exit transition all stay the same.
- **Accessibility:** the steps card was the polite live region. Without it, announce "Loading LyniaGo" once when `loading` starts. The slow pill (status) and offline panel (alert) still announce as before.
- **Reduced motion:** nothing else changes.
- Delete the step-label strings from copy (`src/boot/splash/*` copy and any `SP` keys). Keep them only if the rider splash still uses them.
