Anatomy, top → bottom

  1. Mint header — --accent-wash, square bottom edge, padding-bottom: 24. Greeting 25px/700 ink, time-aware (morning <12 · afternoon <18 · evening after); flat sun sticker 46px (moon after 18:00); 42px white circle bell with a gold unread dot. No avatar button. Address row 11px below the greeting: 13px map-pin + the user's current detected location (reverse-geocoded street address), 12.5px/600 --accent-text, chevron-down; tap → address/location sheet. This is a live GPS value, not a saved profile address; on a denied permission fall back to the last known address with a “set location” prompt. Search bar: white, radius 12 (not a pill), padding 10×15, 12.5px muted placeholder — deliberately quiet, it must not outweigh the greeting or the tiles.
  2. Service tiles — grid of 3 equal columns, gap 8, padding 14 16 0. Tile radius 16, padding 10 4 9, column layout, gap 6, no shadow. Hard rules

  • Zero box-shadows anywhere on this screen (including Android elevation) — depth comes from tint and hairline borders.
  • Gold (--highlight) appears ONLY on: the unread dot, the SOON chip, the ETA chip, star ratings.
  • Service labels are 12px/400 regular, never bold.
  • Icons are the shipped SVG files (assets/service-icons/) — used verbatim, never redrawn or tinted.
  • Type is Inter; every number renders tabular-lining.
